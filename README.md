# Ethiopian Signature Coffee

> **Beyond Coffee, Building Coffee Businesses**

Discover the excellence of Ethiopian coffee. We bring together premium Ethiopian coffee, professional barista and customer service training, coffee business consultancy, and innovative café solutions.

---

## Brand & Core Mission
- **Brand**: Ethiopian Signature Coffee
- **Mission**: To promote Ethiopian coffee culture and empower coffee businesses to achieve excellence.
- **Slogan**: Beyond Coffee, Building Coffee Businesses.

---

## Core Capabilities & Services
1. **Premium Ethiopian Coffee**: Specialty coffee sourcing, single-origin lots, and direct trade relationships.
2. **Coffee Business Consultancy**: Turnkey café setups, operational systems, and business development.
3. **Professional Training**: Comprehensive barista certification, sensory analysis, and hospitality service standards.
4. **Menu & Recipe Development**: Signature espresso drinks, traditional Ethiopian coffee recipes, and menu engineering.
5. **Coffee Business Solutions**: Workflow optimization, equipment curation, and profitability improvement.

---

## Key Features & Design System
- **Responsive Across All Devices**: Mobile-first design optimized for compact phones, standard smartphones, tablets, and desktop displays.
- **Brand Colors**: Ethiopian national heritage palette (Forest Green `#0b4627`, Warm Gold `#d48800`, Crimson Accent `#c8102e`) on a clean, breathable white backdrop.
- **Typography**: Cinzel (Serif) & Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Direct Connect**: Instant WhatsApp advisory integration and full touch navigation.

---

## Getting Started Locally
To run the website locally:

```bash
# Using Python
python -m http.server 8000

# Or with Node
npx serve .
```

Open [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) in your web browser.
